## Selected work

### [Honesty Tab](https://github.com/ZacMossHK/honest-tab-local) — guest-facing POS terminal
Sole developer. In production at a climbing guesthouse near Malaga, where it has
tracked thousands of orders and taken over 2,000 card payments with no staff
involvement. Offline-first: a local PostgreSQL database keeps serving guests
through network outages and synchronises to legacy Google Sheets records,
treating the cloud data as the source of truth. End-to-end regression tests in
Cypress, containerised with Docker for parity between macOS development and
Linux production.

`Python` `Reflex` `PostgreSQL` `Stripe API` `Cypress` `Docker`

### [Betabook](https://github.com/ZacMossHK/betabook) — mobile app for rock climbers
Helps climbers record and recall their moves on a climb. Includes a pan and zoom
image manipulation tool with gesture control, animating over 100 individual
elements at a consistent 60fps. Distributed to testers in the UK, Europe and the
United States and iterated on their feedback.

`TypeScript` `React Native` `Expo`

### [Remember to Vote](https://github.com/ZacMossHK/remember-to-vote) — polling station reminder service
[remembertovote.org.uk](https://remembertovote.org.uk) · Built for
[Campaign Lab](https://www.campaignlab.uk/). Signs voters up to
receive their polling station address by text on election day.

`TypeScript` `React` `Next.js` `Node.js` `Vercel` `Jest` `Twilio API`
